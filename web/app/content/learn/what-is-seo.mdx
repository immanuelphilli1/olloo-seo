SEO (search engine optimization) is the work of getting your website to show up when people search for things related to your business. It covers Google, Bing and now AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity. The traffic it brings is free, steady and comes from people who are already looking for what you offer.

## What SEO means in plain English

SEO is the process of making your pages easy for search engines to find, understand and trust, so they show your pages in the free (organic) results. The ads at the top of Google are paid. Everything below them is earned through SEO.

Surfer's SEO course offers a modern way to think about it: "search everywhere optimization." People no longer search only on Google. They ask ChatGPT, read Google AI Overviews and check Perplexity. The goal is the same in every place. You want your business to appear when someone looks for the problem you solve.

## How search engines work: crawl, index, rank

Google finds and shows pages in three steps: crawling, indexing and ranking.

### Crawling

Google sends automated bots, called crawlers or spiders, to read pages across the web. They start from a list of known URLs. Then they follow links to find new pages. This is why links between pages matter so much.

### Indexing

Google stores what its crawlers find in a giant library called the index. Ahrefs' SEO course compares a search engine to a library that stores copies of web pages instead of books. If your page is not in the index, it cannot show up in search. No amount of good writing will fix that.

### Ranking

When someone searches, Google looks through its index and picks the most relevant, trustworthy and useful pages. The order it shows them in is the ranking. Google uses hundreds of signals. According to Ahrefs, Google tweaks its algorithm 500 to 600 times per year, so nobody outside Google knows exactly how it works.

## Why SEO is worth your time

SEO is worth doing because it brings visitors who are actively looking for you, at no cost per click. Ahrefs' course names three reasons marketers choose it.

1. **Search traffic is free.** You do not pay for each visit like you do with ads.
2. **Search traffic is consistent.** Social posts and emails cause short spikes that fade. Search demand for a topic stays fairly steady month to month.
3. **The audience is huge.** Most internet users use Google.

There is also a fourth reason: intent. Someone who searches "best proposal software for agencies" has a problem and wants an answer now. A social ad interrupts people. A search result meets them at the moment they need you.

SEO is not dead because of AI. According to Surfer's SEO course, Google still sends 190 times more traffic to websites than ChatGPT. Search is changing, but it is not shrinking.

## What makes a page rank in Google

Three factors matter most for beginners: backlinks, search intent and content depth.

### Backlinks

A backlink is a link from another website to your page. Think of each one as a vote of trust. Google's own "How Search Works" page says that if prominent websites link to a page, that is a good sign the information is trusted. An Ahrefs study of over 1 billion pages found a clear link between backlinks from unique websites and organic traffic.

### Search intent

Search intent is the reason behind a search. Google wants to show the result that best fits that reason. You can see the intent by searching your keyword and looking at the top results. For "slow cooker recipes," the top results are recipe lists. For "slow cooker," they are store pages. A store page will not rank for the first search, and a recipe post will not rank for the second. Our guide to [search intent and the 3 C's](/learn/search-intent) shows how to read it.

### Content depth

Content depth means fully answering the question. Depth is not the same as length. Ahrefs points out that the top page for "how to turn off iPhone 12" was only 185 words. It ranked because it solved the problem from start to finish.

## How SEO connects to AI search

Good SEO is the foundation for showing up in AI answers too. AI assistants often search the web before they answer. Surfer's course notes that ChatGPT uses Bing's index when it searches. Ahrefs found that 76% of AI Overview citations came from pages already in Google's top 10, though a later study put that figure closer to 38%.

The work that helps you rank also helps you get cited. That means clear content, trusted mentions and a site that crawlers can read. One new step is checking that AI crawlers like GPTBot are not blocked. You can test that with the free [AI crawler checker](/tools/ai-crawler-checker). ## How to start doing SEO today

The fastest way to start is to fix what is broken, then build pages people are searching for. Follow these steps in order.

1. **Set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Both are free. They show which searches bring you visitors and which pages have problems.
2. **Run an audit.** [The free {site.name} audit](/) checks your site for common Google and AI search issues and writes the fix for each one. Your {site.brand} SEO Score gives you a simple baseline to improve on.
3. **Find your money pages.** These are the pages that turn visitors into customers. Make sure they exist and are optimized first.
4. **Pick 10 to 15 keywords.** Choose ones with real demand, value to your business and intent you can match.
5. **Create or improve one page at a time.** Match the intent, write a clear title and link to it from related pages.
6. **Earn links and mentions.** Share your best work with people who would find it useful.

Expect SEO to take months, not days. Surfer's 30-day plan says it plainly: you probably will not see results in 30 days, but you will have the foundation in place.

## FAQ

### How long does SEO take to work?

SEO usually takes months to show clear results. In the first few weeks, look for early signals in Google Search Console. Watch for rising impressions, new keywords and positions moving up, even before clicks grow.

### Is SEO free?

Search traffic is free because you do not pay per click. SEO itself takes time, and some people pay for tools or help. You can start with free tools like Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and the free {site.name} audit.

### Is SEO dead because of AI?

No. Surfer's course reports that Google still sends 190 times more traffic to websites than ChatGPT. AI assistants also lean on search indexes like Bing to find pages, so strong SEO helps you show up in AI answers too.

### What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in a list of search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) helps your brand get mentioned and cited inside AI answers. AEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it.
